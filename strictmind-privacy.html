1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that we request will be retained by us and used as described in this privacy policy.

A. Accessibility Service API (Important)

StrictMind uses the Android AccessibilityService API to provide its core functionality.

  • Purpose: To detect in real-time when a "locked" or "protected" application (such as a trading app) is launched by the user.
  • Data Usage: The service only monitors the package name of the foreground application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, keystrokes, or screen content through the Accessibility Service. All processing happens locally on your device.
  • User Control: This permission is required for the app's locking feature to work. You can disable it at any time in your device settings.

B. Device Administrator

This app uses the Device Administrator permission.

  • Purpose: To prevent the unauthorized uninstallation of StrictMind while an active lock session is in progress. This ensures the user's discipline goals are maintained.

C. Installed Applications

StrictMind retrieves a list of installed applications on your device.

  • Purpose: To allow you to select which apps you wish to protect or lock. This data never leaves your device.

D. Camera Access

The app requires camera access to scan QR Codes for "Guardian Pairing" and "Emergency Unlock" features. Data is processed locally and never stored.
